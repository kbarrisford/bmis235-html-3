Weather Conditions

Winter brings changing conditions to the Northern California coast, and understanding the weather can make a big difference when planning a crabbing trip. For the 2026–2027 winter, NOAA's seasonal outlook favors warmer-than-average temperatures across much of the western United States, with an increased likelihood of above-average precipitation across California. These are seasonal probabilities, not guarantees of what any particular weekend will bring. Rainstorms can increase freshwater runoff, reduce water clarity, and create challenging conditions near river mouths and coastal areas. Strong winds, large swells, and powerful currents can also make shore crabbing dangerous and affect how safely crab gear can be deployed and retrieved. Between storm systems, calmer weather and manageable ocean conditions may provide better opportunities to get out on the water. Tides are another important part of planning. Changing tidal levels and currents can influence where and when you set your gear, so checking local tide predictions alongside the marine forecast is essential. Before heading out, review the latest weather, swell, wind, and tide information for your specific location.
